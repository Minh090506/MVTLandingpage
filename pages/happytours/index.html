Day 1 — Hanoi Arrival

Meet & greet at Noi Bai airport with visa-on-arrival service. Private transfer to your hotel in the Old Quarter. Free evening to explore the capital at your pace.

Day 2 — Hanoi → Halong / Lan Ha Bay

Private car to Halong (no shared bus). Embark on overnight cruise in Lan Ha Bay (Halong's quieter twin). Lunch and dinner served on deck. Sunset cocktails over the limestone karsts.

Day 3 — Lan Ha Bay → Hanoi

Sunrise over aquamarine waters. Morning kayaking or bay tour. Late morning disembark, transfer back to Hanoi.

Day 4 — Hanoi City Tour → Phu Quoc

Highlights of Hanoi (Tran Quoc Pagoda, Ho Chi Minh Complex, Temple of Literature). Lunch at a local restaurant, then afternoon flight to Phu Quoc Island.

Day 5 — Phu Quoc Leisure

Full day on your own — couples spa, beach, or sunset cocktails.

Day 6 — Phu Quoc Leisure

Second day at leisure. Optional 4-Island speedboat tour (Coral Garden, May Rut, Gam Ghi, Fingernail) or relax at the resort.

Day 7 — Phu Quoc → Ho Chi Minh

Leisurely morning. Transfer to airport for flight to Ho Chi Minh City. Check in to hotel, free evening.

Day 8 — Saigon City Tour + Cu Chi Tunnels

Morning: Re-Unification Palace, Notre Dame Cathedral, War Remnants Museum. Afternoon drive to Cu Chi to explore the tunnel network.

Day 9 — Ho Chi Minh → Mekong Delta

Day trip to Ben Tre. Boat trip along the Mekong River, visit a family-run business (coconut candy or rice paper workshop). Return to Saigon.

Day 10 — Departure

Free time in Saigon until departure transfer to Tan Son Nhat airport.
